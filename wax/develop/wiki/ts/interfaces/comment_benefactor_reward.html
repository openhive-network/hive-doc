    Interface comment_benefactor_reward

    Related to comment_operation and comment_options_operation. Generated during block processing after cashout time passes and comment is eligible for rewards (nonzero reward). Note: the reward is a fragment of the author portion of comment reward depending on share assigned to benefactor in comment options (can be zero due to rounding).
